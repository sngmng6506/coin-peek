Coin Peek / Privacy

Privacy and data handling

Coin Peek needs no sign-up and no exchange account. Your watchlist and holdings stay on your computer.

Stored on your computer

The following is stored on your computer with electron-store.

  • Watchlist coins (exchange and ticker) and their order
  • Amounts and average prices
  • Panel position, monitor and left or right edge
  • Language and whether the welcome screen was shown

Network requests

  • Prices: requests go directly to the public market data APIs of Upbit (api.upbit.com) and Binance (data-api.binance.vision, falling back to api.binance.com). Requests contain only the tickers being looked up.
  • Update checks: GitHub Releases is checked for new versions.
  • Daily user count: once a day the app downloads a 1-byte file from the GitHub release. Nothing is sent; only the download count is tallied.

The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.
