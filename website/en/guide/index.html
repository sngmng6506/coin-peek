What is Coin Peek?

Coin Peek is a free Windows and Mac desktop widget for checking your coins without switching windows. It works the same way as its stock sibling, Stock Peek.

In one sentence

A free, open-source Windows and Mac widget that shows Upbit and Binance coin prices, a 90-day trend and profit and loss when you move the mouse to the left or right screen edge.

Features

  • The panel slides in when the mouse reaches the screen edge and hides when you move away.
  • Search Upbit’s KRW market by English or Korean name or ticker, and Binance’s USDT market by ticker. Binance results are ranked by 24-hour volume.
  • Enter an amount (up to eight decimals) and an average price to see profit and loss.
  • Small-cap coins show four significant digits (for example $0.00000485).
  • A small chart shows the last 90 days of daily closes.
  • Put the panel on the left or right edge of any monitor.
  • Lives in the system tray (menu bar on Mac) and can start with your computer.

Install and use

  1. Download: Download the latest installer from this site or GitHub Releases: .exe for Windows, .dmg for Mac.
  2. Add coins: Press ＋, choose Upbit or Binance, and search by name or ticker. Optionally enter your amount and average price.
  3. Check at the edge: Move the mouse to the screen edge to open the panel. Move away and it hides.

The app is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. On Mac it is not notarized; if the first launch is blocked, click “Open Anyway” in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Price sources and refresh

Prices come directly from the public market data APIs of Upbit and Binance. No API key or exchange account is used. Crypto trades around the clock, so prices refresh every 3 seconds, with one request per exchange for all your coins.

The change figure differs by exchange: Upbit measures against the previous close (00:00 KST), Binance against 24 hours ago. Both charts show the last 90 days of daily closes.

Privacy and local storage

It never logs in to an exchange or places orders. Your watchlist, amounts, average prices, language and panel position are stored on your computer. Details are on the privacy page.

FAQ

Is Coin Peek free?
Yes. There are no ads, payments or sign-ups in the app, and the full source code is MIT-licensed.
Which coins are supported?
Coins listed on Upbit’s KRW market and Binance’s USDT market. Delisted or suspended coins cannot be loaded.
Can it show stocks?
For stocks, use Stock Peek by the same developer. The two apps are installed and run separately.
Does it work on a work computer?
It uses the system proxy and certificates, but if your company network blocks exchange sites, prices cannot load.
Can I trade on these prices?
The prices are not official trade data or investment advice. Check the exchange for the latest price before trading.
